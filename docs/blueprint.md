# **App Name**: Hamraz AI

## Core Features:

- Intelligent Chat: An AI companion named Hamraz, capable of Persian and English conversation with memory, emotional support, and advice. The tool will make reasoning to decide when to use external sources.
- Personalized Language Tutor: AI tutor offering customized Persian/English language lessons and conversation practice. Also helps with language assignments, especially for students in grades 6-12. It will act as a personal tutor and practice lessons with them.
- Real-time Translation: Voice and text translation between Persian and English.
- Reward system: Earn time with AI by earning points (100 points = 20 minutes).
- Online Chat Community: Platform for users to connect, chat, and play games. The app is suitable for people 12 years and older, and +18 conversations are prohibited.
- Simple Games: A selection of simple, engaging games with a point system to increase engagement and earn AI usage time.
- Two-Player Games: Offer 1-on-1 games between community members for double point rewards to improve AI time and language skills.
- Selectable AI Avatars: Users can choose from several male and female AI avatars as friends and teachers.
- Expressive Avatar Reactions: Avatars use body language and facial expressions to convey emotions like affection, surprise, sadness, excitement, empathy, anger, and even love. The avatars will move their lips when they speak.
- Privacy Assurance: The app assures users that their secrets are kept according to privacy policies.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A warm, inviting purple (#9D5FFF) to inspire connection.
- Background color: Light, desaturated purple (#F0EAFF) for a calming atmosphere.
- Accent color: A soft pink (#FF5FD1) for highlighting key interactions and elements.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a modern, friendly sans-serif font.
- Friendly, rounded icons to enhance the user experience.
- Clean, modern layout with clear information hierarchy and engaging visual elements.
- Subtle transitions and feedback animations to create a fluid and interactive experience.
